# DalalVue v1.0 MVP scope

## Decision

DalalVue v1.0 is a public, no-account Indian market research website for beginners and active traders. It provides official TradingView BSE end-of-day chart widgets, explainable general market analysis based only on permitted DalalVue inputs, normalized Indian financial and policy news, and public SEO pages.

## Included

- English and Hindi interface and core content.
- Supported BSE equities and indices available through the TradingView widget market list.
- Search, instrument pages, responsive TradingView charts, company information, and approved DalalVue content.
- Bullish, bearish, and neutral analysis for documented timeframes.
- Evidence, timestamp, methodology, and notice for every signal.
- Normalized company, finance, RBI, government, policy, and relevant political news.
- Public archives and search-engine controls.
- Anonymous AI usage with quotas and abuse protection.
- Monitoring, backups, support, correction, and incident processes.

## Excluded

Broker integrations, order execution, personalized advice, user accounts, cloud portfolios, alerts, social posting, guaranteed predictions, NSE/raw exchange-data ingestion, and real-time data unless separately licensed and approved.

## Launch gates

Public launch requires confirmation that the official TradingView widget is used within its current terms, visible TradingView attribution, written news/content permissions, legal review, published notices and privacy documents, security testing, AI safety review, operational monitoring, rollback, and a tested widget/provider outage response.
