# DalalVue fast public MVP scope

## Decision

DalalVue’s fast public MVP is a public, no-account Indian market education and chart-discovery website for beginners and active traders. It provides official TradingView widgets for supported international instruments, basic instrument metadata, educational content, and clear source/delay notices.

## Included

- English interface and a small reviewed Hindi landing/navigation surface.
- Supported international equities, ETFs, and indices available through the TradingView widget market list, starting with one documented market region.
- Search, instrument pages, responsive TradingView charts, company information, and approved DalalVue content.
- Chart-source, attribution, exchange, timezone, and delay notices.
- Basic public SEO controls for approved static and instrument pages.
- Monitoring, support, correction, and provider-outage processes.

## Excluded

- Indian exchange-data ingestion.
- DalalVue-owned live or delayed quote APIs.
- Bullish, bearish, or neutral public signals.
- AI analysis and anonymous AI prompts.
- News ingestion, summarization, and public archives.
- Broker integrations, order execution, personalized advice, user accounts, cloud portfolios, alerts, social posting, guaranteed predictions, and crypto.

## Launch gates

Public launch requires confirmation that the official TradingView widget is used within its current terms, visible TradingView attribution, an approved international symbol list, legal review of wording and scope, published notices and privacy documents, security testing, operational monitoring, correction/support contact, rollback, and a tested widget/provider outage response.
