# Fast public MVP release acceptance specification

## Product

- A user in India can find a supported international instrument and understand exchange, timezone, source, delay, and timestamp.
- A supported international instrument page renders the official TradingView widget across desktop and mobile states.
- TradingView attribution and the link remain visible.
- No application code, backend job, AI prompt, or analytics pipeline extracts TradingView widget data.
- No public signal, AI answer, or news summary is exposed in the fast public MVP.
- English core flows and the reviewed Hindi landing/navigation surface are usable.

## Safety and compliance

- The fast public MVP does not accept personalized trade requests.
- Stale or missing data is visible.
- No public page exposes secrets, private data, unlicensed raw content, or extracted TradingView data.
- Legal, TradingView terms, privacy, security, and copyright reviews are recorded.

## Operations

- Widget outage, rollback, and alert paths are tested.
- Logs and metrics support investigation without storing unnecessary sensitive prompts.
- SEO controls prevent private, duplicate, stale, and unsupported pages from indexing.

## Release decision

Any failed compliance, data-rights, security, or privacy gate blocks public release even if the UI is complete.
