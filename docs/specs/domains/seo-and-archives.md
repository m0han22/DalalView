# SEO and public archives specification

## Goal

Make useful public market and news pages discoverable without indexing private, duplicate, stale, or unsupported content.

## Requirements

- Stable canonical URLs for instruments, sectors, events, and archives.
- Server-rendered or pre-rendered public content where practical.
- Accurate title, description, Open Graph, structured data, sitemap, robots, and canonical metadata.
- Visible source, timestamp, delay, methodology, and AI labels.
- `noindex` for private routes, query duplicates, thin pages, and unsupported content.
- Archive pages with correction and freshness behavior.
- Performance, mobile usability, accessibility, and multilingual metadata.

SEO copy must not use sensational certainty or unsupported causal claims. AI pages require quality checks before indexing at scale.
