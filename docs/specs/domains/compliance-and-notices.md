# Compliance and notices specification

## Purpose

Keep public content within the approved general-information and education boundary until legal counsel confirms any broader classification.

## Requirements

- Never present an output as a command to buy, sell, or hold.
- Define bullish, bearish, and neutral as time-bound analytical states.
- Show evidence, timestamp, data freshness, methodology link, and uncertainty.
- Do not promise returns, certainty, targets, or guaranteed predictions.
- Display notices at the point of chart, signal, AI, and news use; do not rely on a footer alone.
- Publish terms, privacy, AI-use disclosure, data-source attribution, copyright contact, correction process, and complaints contact.
- Review marketing, SEO titles, translations, and social copy under the same rules.
- Record legal review, approved wording, owner, and effective date.

## Acceptance scenarios

**Given** a user opens a signal, **when** it is displayed, **then** timeframe, evidence, timestamp, methodology, and informational notice are visible.

**Given** a user asks for a personalized buy recommendation, **when** the AI responds, **then** it refuses the instruction and provides general evidence-based context.

**Given** a data timestamp is stale, **when** a quote or chart is shown, **then** the stale state is prominent and currentness is not implied.

## Gate

No public launch or material signal change without India-focused legal review.
